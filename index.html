How to use Board

  1. Everything stays on your device. Board runs entirely in your browser — your boards are never uploaded, no account needed.
  2. Multiple projects. Use the project switcher (top bar) to create and switch between separate boards — each keeps its own columns, cards and colors.
  3. Add columns and cards, then drag cards between columns (or drag a column's header to reorder columns).
  4. Click a card to set a description, a start/due date (click the field to open a calendar), a checklist, a cover image, or a color.
  5. Shuffle colors from the top bar any time you like — and the current board also reshuffles itself automatically the first time you open it each day.
  6. Timeline view lays dated cards out on a horizontal calendar, spanning from start to due date — drag a bar's edges to trim it, or its middle to move it. Switch the project filter to see one board or every project at once.
  7. Today view is a daily to-do list separate from your boards: search for any card across every project and add it to today's list, then check it off as you go. It resets itself each morning.
  8. Random Ideas is a quick capture list — drop any idea in, tag and describe it, then send it to your active board as a task whenever you're ready to work on it.
  9. Done cards archive themselves the day after you check them off — find them again anytime from the Archive button, where you can restore or delete them.
  10. Nothing is saved to a file automatically. Use Export (JSON) (Cmd/Ctrl+S, in this menu) to download a backup, and Import (JSON) to restore it later.
